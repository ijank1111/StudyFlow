# StudyFlow

A smart academic planning application designed to help students organize their assignments and manage their study schedules effectively.

## Features

- **Smart Scheduling**: Automatically generates task breakdowns based on assignment briefs.
- **Visual Task Board**: Organize ideas and sub-tasks using a flexible sticky-note system.
- **Friend Sharing**: Share study plans with your friends to collaborate and stay motivated.
- **Personalized Preferences**: Configure study hours and days off to fit your lifestyle.
